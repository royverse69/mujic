Privacy policy

Mujic Dloader is designed as a lightweight browser-based tool.

  • Search terms and requests are sent to the configured music backend and its upstream provider to retrieve results and audio.
  • A small local browser setting remembers recent backend readiness for up to 10 minutes. Search history is not intentionally stored by this page.
  • When you download a track, the browser processes audio and metadata locally. The MP3 encoder is loaded from a public CDN when needed.
  • This page does not include analytics or advertising code. Hosting providers, backend, upstream providers, and CDN may independently process technical request data under their own policies.
  • Do not enter passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information into the search field.

This app relies on external services and cannot guarantee their privacy practices or availability.

Help & errors

  • Search fails: check your connection and wait for the backend to wake. A CORS or upstream error may also prevent results.
  • Download fails: the source may be unavailable, return an invalid stream, or block browser access to audio/artwork.
  • MP3 conversion fails: use current Chrome or Edge and keep the page open. Large tracks need extra memory, and the encoder needs internet on first use.
  • Year or duration is missing: the catalog may not supply that detail. The app does not invent missing values.
  • Cover art is missing: the source may not provide a usable image or the browser may be blocked from fetching it.
  • Bulk downloads: your browser may ask permission for multiple downloads. Allow them if you intend to download selected tracks.
