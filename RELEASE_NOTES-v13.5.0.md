# Mujic v13.5.0

- **Desktop mini-player, Spotify style** – flush, opaque 88px bar split 30 / 40 / 30: now playing | previous, play, next with a seek bar and times | PiP, volume and a new inline volume slider. No translucent tint from the page behind it and no backdrop blur.
- **Progress bar fix** – in waveform mode the mini progress fill was hidden and the mini waveform canvas does not exist, so the bar vanished. The fill now always shows.
- **Settings** – category icons follow the theme accent instead of rainbow colours; one control column width; rows align on a single grid.
- **Minimal copy** – settings descriptions cut to one short line (or removed); hub intro and sub-page paragraphs removed; Radio, All artists, playlist-sheet and About text shortened.
- **Tab title** – shows the original Mujic title whenever playback is paused or stopped.
- **Quick Picks on desktop** – click-and-drag scrolling and arrow buttons. The "Swipe Right" hint is retired for good after 5 real swipes.
- **Suggested for You → Show more** – expands the list in place (Show less collapses it). It used to run a search for the words "songs for you".
